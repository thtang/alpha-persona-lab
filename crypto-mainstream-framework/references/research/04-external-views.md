# 04 - External Views And Criticism

## Supportive Adoption Signals

- SEC allowed listing/trading of spot Bitcoin ETPs on January 10, 2024, creating a regulated access wrapper for Bitcoin. Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023
- BlackRock IBIT markets itself as exposure to Bitcoin through an exchange-traded product, reducing operational and custody complexity. Source: https://www.ishares.com/us/products/333011/ishares-bitcoin-trust-etf
- Chainalysis 2025 index added institutional activity above $1M, reflecting professional/institutional participation, and listed India, the U.S., Pakistan, Vietnam, and Brazil as top adoption countries. Source: https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-global-crypto-adoption-index/
- Visa Onchain Analytics frames stablecoins as fiat-backed tokens moving on public chains and tracks supply, volume, and addresses. Source: https://visaonchainanalytics.com/
- DefiLlama showed total stablecoin market cap around $315B at the time checked on 2026-06-20. Source: https://defillama.com/stablecoins

## Regulatory Support With Guardrails

- White House GENIUS Act fact sheet says the law creates the first federal regulatory system for stablecoins, requiring liquid reserve backing and monthly reserve disclosures. Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-genius-act-into-law/
- U.S. Treasury proposed rules to implement GENIUS Act illicit-finance requirements, including AML/sanctions obligations. Source: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0435

## Critical And Risk Views

- SEC approval statement explicitly says the action was limited to ETPs holding Bitcoin and did not approve or endorse Bitcoin or crypto trading platforms. Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023
- Federal Reserve FEDS Note says stablecoins grew about 50% in market cap during 2025 but also increase interconnections with traditional finance and may amplify run and stability risks. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/stablecoins-in-2025-developments-and-financial-stability-implications-20260408.html
- BIS emphasizes stablecoin reserve quality, transparency, AML/CFT, and global coordination risks. Source: https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2025e3.htm
- FSB warns crypto-assets and stablecoins could become a threat to global financial stability as scale and interconnections grow. Source: https://www.fsb.org/work-of-the-fsb/financial-innovation-and-structural-change/crypto-assets-and-global-stablecoins/

## Balanced Interpretation

The outside view supports a "yes, but segmented" answer: mainstream rails are forming, especially for Bitcoin exposure and stablecoin settlement, but official institutions remain focused on investor protection, financial stability, AML/CFT, market integrity, and regulatory consistency.
