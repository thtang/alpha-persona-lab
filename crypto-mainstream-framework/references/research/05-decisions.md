# 05 - Adoption Decisions And Market Structure Events

## Bitcoin ETF Approval

On 2024-01-10 the SEC approved listing and trading of spot Bitcoin ETP shares. This was a market-structure decision, not an endorsement of Bitcoin. It opened a familiar brokerage wrapper and accelerated the "wrapper-first" adoption path.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023

## IBIT Scale

BlackRock's IBIT page checked on 2026-06-20 showed a digital-asset ETP with net assets over $48B as of 2026-06-18, a 0.25% sponsor fee, and tens of millions of shares in daily volume. This is concrete evidence that Bitcoin exposure has entered mainstream ETF plumbing.

Source: https://www.ishares.com/us/products/333011/ishares-bitcoin-trust-etf

## GENIUS Act

The GENIUS Act was signed on 2025-07-18 and created a federal stablecoin framework emphasizing reserves, disclosures, marketing rules, and holder priority in insolvency. This is a permissioning event for payment-stablecoin adoption.

Sources:
- https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-genius-act-into-law/
- https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0435

## Payment And Brokerage Integration

Federal Reserve research notes stablecoins' growing ties to payment networks, banks, retail apps, and brokerage funding, including stablecoin transfer and account funding use cases. This is the shift from crypto-native use to mainstream plumbing.

Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/stablecoins-in-2025-developments-and-financial-stability-implications-20260408.html

## On-chain Data And Institutional Flow

The Alex Svanevik/Nansen transcript notes weak retail activity but stronger institutional and high-net-worth flows, and frames on-chain analytics as a way to verify rather than guess. This supports a corpus rule: do not infer adoption from social sentiment alone.
