## Repeated Claims Across Sources

1. Bitcoin's most credible mainstream path is institutional wrapper adoption, not immediate everyday-payment replacement.
2. Stablecoins have a clearer utility path than most crypto assets because they move dollars across public chains.
3. Tokenization is a staged migration: cash/stablecoins first, then Treasuries/equities, then harder real-world assets.
4. Regulation is both adoption unlock and constraint: it opens institutional doors while imposing reserve, disclosure, custody, AML/CFT, and marketing rules.
5. Mainstreaming increases systemic-plumbing risks rather than eliminating crypto risks.

## Key Distinction

"Blockchain utility" and "token investment value" are separate. A rail can be mainstream while many tokens fail to capture value.
