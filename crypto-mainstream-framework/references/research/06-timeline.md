## Latest Dynamic As Of 2026-06-21

- Bitcoin is no longer only a crypto-native asset; regulated ETF access and large issuer products show portfolio-wrapper mainstreaming.
- Stablecoins are the strongest real-utility crypto lane, but regulation and reserve/run risk are now central.
- Tokenization is moving from slogan to staged implementation: cash/stablecoins first, then Treasuries/equities, then harder assets.
- Institutional adoption does not eliminate retail risk; it changes the risk surface from "can I buy it?" to "what wrapper, custody, liquidity, tax, and counterparty structure am I using?"
