# 05 - Decisions / Case Logic

Research date: 2026-07-06

## Case Logic Extracted From Public Reconstructions

### AXTI / InP Substrates

Decision pattern:
- Start from AI optical bandwidth pressure.
- Move upstream from modules to lasers to InP substrate.
- Ask whether substrate supply is concentrated, hard to expand, and under-covered by the market.

Lesson:
- Upstream material constraints can reprice before revenue is obvious.
- Once the market learns the bottleneck, future returns require financial verification.

### AAOI / Optical Modules

Decision pattern:
- Qualification -> orders -> capacity ramp -> revenue and gross margin.
- Do not stop at "AI optical" label; inspect customer/order and margin proof.

Lesson:
- Current orders matter, but gross margin and capacity utilization decide durability.

### AI Cloud / Neocloud

Decision pattern:
- GPUs are not enough.
- Check power access, customer contracts, financing, utilization, and software stack.

Lesson:
- Capex-heavy companies can be right direction / wrong stock if dilution or debt captures the upside.

### BBU / High-Power Cylindrical Cells

Decision pattern:
- AI racks draw short bursts of power and need voltage stability.
- Rack-level BBU adoption shifts attention to compact high-current cylindrical cells.
- If Samsung SDI/Panasonic cells are short and Simplo is qualified as pack assembler, the scarce layer is not "generic battery" but BBU-grade cylindrical cells and pack qualification.

Evidence:
- The Elec reported Samsung SDI supplies cells to Simplo, which assembles BBUs for North American tech customers including Amazon and Meta.
- The Elec also reported Samsung SDI's 21700 40V3 BBU cell mass production plan in Malaysia.

Failure conditions:
- Hyperscalers dual-source enough cells quickly.
- BBU demand proves too small relative to company revenue.
- Shortage is solved before price/margin/revenue shows up.
- Market already prices in the entire BBU chain.
