## Conversation Patterns

### Beginner Translation

Bonnie frequently asks guests to explain mechanisms for normal people: what happens when a retail buyer buys a Bitcoin ETF share, what stablecoins actually do, why institutions care, and what risks retail investors miss.

### Infrastructure Over Price

Krista Lynch frames ETF and tokenization progress as market infrastructure build rather than only token price appreciation. Michael Lau similarly argues that short-term returns are insufficient and ecosystem health should be read through capital, technology, and culture.

### Institutionalization Is Ambivalent

The corpus treats institutional adoption as both bullish and maturity-inducing. ETFs open access for people who cannot or will not manage wallets, but also create market-maker, hedging, custody, and regulatory dependencies.

### Stablecoins Are The Practical Use Case

Multiple transcripts converge on stablecoins as the first obvious mass-market utility: faster, cheaper, more accessible dollar transfer and settlement. The stronger claim is not "stablecoins replace the dollar" but "stablecoins are a better format for moving dollars."

### Asia Matters

The Michael Lau transcript emphasizes Hong Kong, Singapore, Thailand, and broader Asian regulatory/capital-flow contexts. The lesson: mainstreaming is jurisdictional and regional, not only U.S.-centric.

## Caution

Many claims are guest views. Attribute to "the transcript/guest argues" unless Bonnie explicitly agrees or frames it as her own conclusion.
