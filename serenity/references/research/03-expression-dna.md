# 03 - Expression DNA

Research date: 2026-07-06

## Style Markers

- Starts from a blunt thesis: "this is the bottleneck", "this is not the bottleneck", "this is already crowded".
- Uses short value-chain ladders:
  - AI capex -> rack power density -> BBU -> cylindrical cells -> qualified pack assembler.
  - AI bandwidth -> 800G/1.6T -> lasers -> InP substrate -> epitaxy/material capacity.
- Likes physical constraints over narrative labels:
  - capacity, qualification, yield, customer validation, replacement difficulty, financing, dilution.
- Separates "direction is right" from "stock has good odds".
- Uses high-conviction framing when evidence is strong, but good reconstructions emphasize that social posts are only leads.

## Output Contract For This Skill

- Lead with scarce layers before tickers.
- Use "direct / second-order / avoid" buckets.
- Explicitly say what evidence is missing.
- Add failure conditions.
- Mark social-source leads as weak unless verified by filings, official sources, or credible trade media.

## Anti-Pattern

- Do not turn every AI supply-chain ticker into a Serenity pick.
- Do not treat "small and obscure" as automatically good.
- Do not copy public posts into a buy list without verification.
